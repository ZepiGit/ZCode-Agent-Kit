## Q2 — ticket 61 changes two row values for slow clients (Round 12 Q3 condition 2, third bullet)

Facts: ticket 61 replaces the proxy's three `tee()` response copies with a pull-on-demand response body (`zcode-proxy-src/src/proxy/response-body.ts`), so the proxy reads the upstream at the client's pace (backpressure). Upstream bytes and client bytes are unchanged (fixture captured on the tip; 8 of 9 mutants killed). For clients that keep up nothing changes. For a client slower than its upstream two values in the request row change meaning: Total (and tok/s) now include the client's drain time, and Tok counts only the events read before a client that leaves early disconnected. Field names and TTFB are unchanged. Round 12 Q3 condition 2's third bullet says such a change "comes back before it ships". Linux S1/stalled peak 175 → 148.5 MB; the windows attribution and windows criteria are pending CI (two measurement branches running: staging/61m1 at 1495c03 and staging/61m2 at the head).

Recommendation: accept: the new values describe what the proxy actually did (it streamed at the client's pace), the row keeps its field names, and the change is documented in the release notes and the row's help text; no config switch. Condition: the release-notes line and the docs say "Total and tok/s include the client's read time when the client reads slower than the upstream; Tok counts events delivered before a client disconnects".
