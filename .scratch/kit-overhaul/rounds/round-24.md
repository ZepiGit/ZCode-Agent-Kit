## Q1 — S5/short after retention

Facts (linux, local): retention (500 finished tasks or 7 days; running and unknown kept; eviction on finish, startup and lookup; files pruned; migration replay byte-identical) works: S5/long (3,000 tasks) tip RED 40.8 %/1k, procPeak 238.6 MB, reboot cost 31 MB, 9,000 files -> branch GREEN 1.11 %/1k, procPeak 111.4 MB, reboot cost 4 MB, 1,500 files; the bridge plateaus at ~104-106 MB. S5/short (400 tasks) never reaches the 500 bound: tip 44.53/41.18/39.60 %/1k, branch 35.29/37.62/34.52 (two of three "GREEN" only on a missed trend flag). Attribution: about half is the growing task history (~4.6 KB JS heap per retained task, ~28 KB RSS after full GC: 69 % V8 old-space pages, 18 % glibc main arena, 14 % other anon); a never-committed cap-50 build keeps RSS-after-GC flat (86.4 -> 87.2 MB) but still shows 11.4-12.8 MB/1k (~12-14 %/1k) in the first 400 tasks = runtime warm-up that flattens after ~500 tasks. Ruled out: MALLOC_ARENA_MAX=1, --v8-pool-size=1, tasks_list response size, O(n) copy/sort, V8 semi-space (ticket 36). Conclusion: no retention bound at or below 500 brings S5/short under 5 %/1k; it needs per-task heap under ~1 KB and the warm-up gone. Ticket 30's own exits (linux: >= 10 fresh runs <= 5 %/1k regardless of trend; windows: round 13's unstable band exit) cannot be met.

Options the implementer names: (a) judge bridge growth after the retention bound (the S5/long shape: warm-up excluded); (b) give the warm-up its own measurement rule or owner; (c) a new ticket to shrink each retained task's footprint (e.g. finished tasks served from disk), which still leaves the warm-up.

Recommendation: (a)+(c). Ticket 30 merges with its retention criteria met and S5/long GREEN; S5/short's growth window starts after a warm-up of 500 tasks (the measurement rule changes, the 5 %/1k budget does not; the scenario runs long enough to have >= 400 post-warm-up tasks); the linux entry and the windows unstable entry move to a new ticket "per-task bridge memory" (option c) with the same exits; 30 closes. If (a)'s post-warm-up window is still over 5 %/1k on the branch, (c)'s ticket owns it with those numbers.
